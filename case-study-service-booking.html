Overview

A service business often loses leads because the booking process is too slow, too messy, or too hard to complete on mobile. This case study format shows how ByteWave Digital would structure a better experience.

The challenge

The business needs a clean way for customers to understand the service, choose a time, submit a request, and receive a clear follow-up without extra friction.

The solution

  • Simple hero section with one primary action.
  • Clear service categories and availability cues.
  • Short enquiry form with structured fields.
  • Admin-friendly confirmation flow after submission.

What this improves

The result is a page that looks more professional, captures more useful leads, and gives the business owner a practical system they can maintain.
